🌍 Air Quality Monitoring Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive, two-page Power BI dashboard designed to analyze air quality metrics, pollutant concentrations, traffic volume impact, and temperature correlations across different seasons and regions.

 📊 Dashboard Overview

 Page 1: Pollutant Overview & Seasonal Trends
* KPI Cards: Tracks key environmental averages across 5,000 total records:
* PM10: 99.88
* PM2.5: 60.26 
* CO (Carbon Monoxide): 1.00
* NO₂ (Nitrogen Dioxide): 39.90
* SO₂ (Sulfur Dioxide): 14.96
* Interactive Slicers:Filter the entire report dynamically by Season(Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter) and Region (Urban, Rural).
* Measures by Date: Multi-line trend analysis tracking pollutant fluctuations over time.
* Measure by Season: 100% stacked bar chart comparing the proportional distribution of pollutants across seasons.
* Measure by Region: Pie chart illustrating the regional split between Urban (50.47% / 158.13K) and Rural (49.53% / 155.21K) areas.

 Page 2: AQI, Traffic Volume & Temperature Correlations
* Measure by AQI: Horizontal bar chart categorizing air quality readings into Moderate, Good, and Poor levels.
* Traffic Volume & PM2.5 by Region: Donut chart and summary table comparing total traffic volume (13.66M total) against cumulative PM2.5 levels (313.34K total) across Urban and Rural zones.
* Measures by Temperature: Four segmented area charts analyzing air quality behavior across specific temperature ranges (14°–20°, 20°–30°, 30°–40°, and 40°–50°).

 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* Power BI Desktop: Data modeling, DAX measures, and report visualization.
* Data Visualization: KPI cards, slicers, line/area charts, 100% stacked bar charts, donut/pie charts, and summary tables.

 💡 Key Insights
* Regional Balance: Pollution distribution is nearly even between Urban (50.47%) and Rural (49.53%) regions, correlating closely with similar traffic volumes (6.84M Urban vs. 6.82M Rural)].
* Air Quality Status: The vast majority of recorded readings fall within the Moderate AQI category, followed by Good, with minimal Poor air quality instances.
* Temperature Impact: Air quality measures peak sharply in the moderate-to-warm temperature bands (20°–30° and 30°–40°) compared to extreme low or high temperatures.
