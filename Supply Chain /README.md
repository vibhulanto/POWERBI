Beauty & Personal Care Supply Chain Performance Dashboard – Power BI

 📊 Project Overview
The Beauty & Personal Care Supply Chain Performance Dashboard is a Power BI data analytics project designed to analyze supply chain performance in the beauty and personal care industry.

The dashboard provides insights into revenue generation, product sales, defect rates, lead times, and manufacturing costs across different product categories, including Cosmetics, Haircare, and Skincare.

The main objective is to monitor key supply chain metrics, evaluate product performance, and support data-driven business decisions.

 🛠️ Tools Used
- Power BI – Interactive dashboard development and data visualization.
- Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation.
- DAX – KPI calculations and measures.
- Excel – Data preparation and analysis.

 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Revenue Generated:$577,604.82
- Average Defect Rate:2.28%
- Average Lead Time:17.08 days
- Total Products Sold:46,099

 📊 Dashboard Features

1. Revenue by Product Type– Compares revenue generated by Cosmetics, Haircare, and Skincare products.
2. Revenue vs Manufacturing Costs– Analyzes revenue and manufacturing costs across different product categories.
3. Supply Chain Performance KPIs – Tracks total revenue, average defect rate, average lead time, and total products sold.
4. Product Category Analysis– Helps identify product categories contributing to overall revenue.

 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze revenue performance across product categories.
- Monitor manufacturing costs and revenue generation.
- Evaluate product defect rates.
- Understand average supply chain lead times.
- Identify high-performing product categories.
- Support business decisions through interactive data visualization.

 💡 Key Insights
- Skincare generates the highest revenue among the three product categories.
- Haircare contributes a moderate share of total revenue.
- Cosmetics generates comparatively lower revenue.
- Skincare has the highest manufacturing costs among the displayed categories.
- The overall average defect rate is 2.28%.
- The average lead time is 17.08 days.

 📚 Key Learnings
- Developing interactive dashboards using Power BI.
- Creating KPI cards using DAX measures.
- Cleaning and transforming data using Power Query.
- Designing comparative charts and visualizations.
- Analyzing revenue and manufacturing cost relationships.
- Presenting business insights through data storytelling.

 📌 Conclusion
This project provides an overview of supply chain performance in the beauty and personal care industry. By analyzing revenue, manufacturing costs, defect rates, lead times, and product sales, the dashboard helps understand business performance and identify opportunities for operational improvement.
